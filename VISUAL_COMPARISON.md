## 🧠 Strategy Application Examples

### Active Recall in Action

**Academic**: "Yesterday we learned about Process Costing, Job Costing, and Marginal Costing in Cost Accounting."

**Professional**: "The new CRM tool has contact management, pipeline tracking, and automated email features."

**Personal**: "This week I learned C major, G major, and D major chords on guitar."

---

### Spaced Repetition in Action

**Academic**:
- Day 1: Learn derivative rules
- Day 3: Practice 10 problems
- Day 7: Mixed practice test
- Day 14: Full mock exam

**Professional**:
- Day 1: Python basics tutorial
- Day 3: Review + new concepts
- Day 7: Build calculator app
- Day 14: Analyze work dataset

**Personal**:
- Day 1: "Bonjour, merci, s'il vous plaît..."
- Day 3: Review + 20 more phrases
- Day 7: Practice with language partner
- Day 14: Order coffee in French

---

### Feynman Technique in Action

**Academic**: "Plants make their own food using sunlight, water, and air. The green parts of the plant catch sunlight and turn it into energy. This process is called photosynthesis and it's how plants grow."

**Professional**: "Cloud computing is like renting a computer that lives on the internet. Instead of buying expensive computers, you pay to use someone else's powerful computers. You can access your files and programs from anywhere."

**Personal**: "To play a guitar chord, you press down certain strings with your fingers on the neck. Then you strum all the strings together with your other hand. The combination of pressed strings makes a nice sound."

---

### 80/20 Rule in Action

**Academic**: ✅ Practice exams > ❌ Re-reading  
*Why*: Exams show what you don't know; re-reading is passive

**Professional**: ✅ Practice core features > ❌ Learn everything  
*Why*: 20% of features handle 80% of tasks

**Personal**: ✅ Common phrases > ❌ Perfect grammar  
*Why*: 100 phrases enable basic conversation

---

### Pomodoro Method in Action

**Academic**:
- 25 min: Active recall flashcards
- 5 min: Walk around, stretch

**Professional**:
- 25 min: Hands-on tool practice
- 5 min: Stand, walk, water

**Personal**:
- 25 min: Sketch portrait features
- 5 min: Eye rest, neck stretches
