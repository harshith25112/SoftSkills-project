# 📋 ALL THREE LEARNING STRATEGY TEST CONTEXTS

Complete test content for comparison and reference.

---

## 📚 CONTEXT 1: ACADEMIC LEARNING

**Target Audience**: Students studying for exams, learning new subjects, mastering academic material

---

### PART A – Active Recall (Short Answer)

**Question**: Without looking at your notes, write down the 3 main concepts you studied yesterday in your most recent subject.

**Type**: Short answer  
**Points**: 20

---

### PART B – Spaced Repetition (Planning Task)

**Task**: You need to master Calculus derivatives for an exam in 2 weeks. Create a 4-step spaced repetition plan:

**Steps**:
1. **Day 1**: First Learning Session
2. **Day 3**: First Review
3. **Day 7**: Second Review
4. **Day 14**: Final Review

**Type**: Planning task  
**Points**: 20

---

### PART C – Feynman Technique (3-Line Explanation)

**Task**: Explain the concept of "photosynthesis" in 3 simple sentences, as if teaching a 10-year-old.

**Type**: Simple explanation  
**Max Lines**: 3  
**Points**: 20

---

### PART D – 80/20 Rule (Multiple Choice)

**Question**: You have 2 hours to prepare for a Biology exam covering 5 chapters. Which task will give you the highest score improvement?

**Options**:
- A. Re-read all 5 chapters from start to finish
- B. Review practice exam questions and past mistakes ✅ **CORRECT**
- C. Rewrite all your notes neatly
- D. Watch YouTube videos summarizing the chapters

**Type**: Multiple choice  
**Points**: 20

---

### PART E – Pomodoro + Active Break (Action Step)

**Task**: Design a 30-minute study session for memorizing Spanish vocabulary:

**Required**:
1. **25-minute focused study activity**: Describe what you'll do
2. **5-minute active break**: Describe physical movement

**Type**: Action planning  
**Points**: 20

## 🎯 DESIGN PRINCIPLES

### 1. **Context Relevance**
Each question is tailored to the specific learning context:
- **Academic**: Exams, lectures, subjects
- **Professional**: Tools, certifications, job skills
- **Personal**: Hobbies, languages, creative skills

### 2. **Real-World Application**
All tasks are based on realistic scenarios:
- ✅ "You have 2 hours to prepare for a Biology exam"
- ✅ "You're learning Python for data analysis"
- ✅ "You want to learn conversational French in one month"

### 3. **Action-Oriented**
Every question requires the learner to DO something:
- ✅ Write from memory
- ✅ Create a plan
- ✅ Explain simply
- ✅ Choose best approach
- ✅ Design a session

### 4. **Duolingo-Style Engagement**
- Short, focused tasks
- Clear instructions
- Immediate feedback
- Progress tracking
- Gamification elements
