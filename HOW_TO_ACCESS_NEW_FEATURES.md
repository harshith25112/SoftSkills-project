## 📍 Step-by-Step Access Guide

### 1. **Open Your Browser**
Go to: `http://localhost:3000`

### 2. **Navigate to Course Catalog**
- Click the **"Course Catalog"** button or menu item
- DO NOT go to any other module

### 3. **Find the Learning Strategy Implementation Card**
Look for this specific course card:
- **Icon**: 🎯
- **Title**: "Learning Strategy Implementation"
- **Description**: "Apply 5 proven learning strategies..."
- **Color**: Indigo to Purple gradient

### 4. **Click to Start**
Click on the **"Learning Strategy Implementation"** card

### 5. **Follow the Flow**
1. **Intro Screen** - Shows 5 strategies → Click "Start Challenge"
2. **Challenge Screen** - Explains what you'll do → Click "Choose Your Learning Context"
3. **Context Selection** - Choose Academic/Professional/Personal
4. **Video Screen** - Watch or skip → Click "Start Test"
5. **Complete All 5 Parts**:
   - Part A: Active Recall
   - Part B: Spaced Repetition
   - Part C: Feynman Technique
   - Part D: 80/20 Rule
   - Part E: Pomodoro Method

### 6. **Results Screen**
After completing all 5 parts, you'll see:
- Your score percentage
- Part breakdown
- **Big button**: "View Success Criteria" ← **CLICK THIS!**

### 7. **Success Criteria Screen** ⭐ NEW FEATURE
You should now see:
- ✅ **CheckCircle icon** (large, green)
- **Title**: "Success Criteria"
- **Your score with 4 level indicators**:
  - 🟢 Excellent (90-100%)
  - 🔵 Good (70-89%)
  - 🟡 Basic (50-69%)
  - 🔴 Needs Review (<50%)
- **Next Steps** section
- **Button**: "Share Your Response" ← **CLICK THIS!**

### 8. **Share Response Screen** ⭐ NEW FEATURE
You should now see:
- ✅ **Share2 icon** (large, indigo)
- **Title**: "Share Your Results"
- **Score display card**
- **4 Social Media Buttons**:
  - 🐦 Share on Twitter
  - 💼 Share on LinkedIn
  - 📘 Share on Facebook
  - 📋 Copy Results to Clipboard

## 🔄 If You Still Don't See It

### Option 1: Hard Refresh
1. Press **Ctrl + Shift + R** (Windows) or **Cmd + Shift + R** (Mac)
2. This clears the browser cache

### Option 2: Clear Cache Completely
1. Press **F12** to open Developer Tools
2. Right-click the refresh button
3. Click "Empty Cache and Hard Reload"

### Option 3: Check You're in the Right Module
Look at the URL in your browser:
- ❌ Wrong: Any other module
- ✅ Correct: Should navigate to Learning Strategies module

### Option 4: Verify Server Restarted
1. Check terminal output
2. Look for: "webpack compiled successfully"
3. Make sure no errors
